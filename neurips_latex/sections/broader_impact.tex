\section{Broader Impact}
The positive intended impact of \framework is better evaluation discipline for
reinforcement-learning safety claims. Compact, executable benchmarks can make
reward misspecification, tampering, and monitor leakage easier to detect before
researchers generalize from unreliable evidence or overstate what a monitor can
actually see.

The main risk is misinterpretation rather than direct misuse. Symbolic exploit
benchmarks can be over-read as evidence about realistic deployments, or used to
optimize against narrow toy failures while leaving harder real-world failures
untouched. We mitigate that risk by keeping the environments small and
instrumented, by making conservative claims about scope, and by reporting
degenerate or leakage-sensitive monitor outcomes instead of presenting
monitoring as solved.
