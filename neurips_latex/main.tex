\begin{abstract}
We present \framework, a compact benchmark and evaluation protocol for diagnosing
specification failures in reinforcement learning. The core contribution is not a
new optimization method, but a task tuple that separates proxy reward, hidden
true objective, corruption channels, monitor channels, distribution shift, and
policy family. We instantiate this tuple in three symbolic environments that
surface proxy gaming, explicit tampering, delayed-cost overoptimization, and
monitorability failures under laptop-scale compute budgets. The package
includes leakage-aware monitor evaluation and strict artifact rules that tie
claims directly to saved outputs. Empirically, \framework exposes large
proxy--true objective gaps and explicit tampering risk across the benchmark,
while leakage-cleared monitor evidence is feasible but narrow, with accepted
results concentrated in SensorTamper-v0.
\end{abstract}
